You’ve been invited to SiteSnap

Someone has invited you to help keep the site record on a job — daily diaries, photos, timesheets and deliveries. To accept, you need the SiteSnap app on your iPhone.

If you are seeing this page, the app isn’t on this device

Invitation links open the SiteSnap app directly when it is installed. This page is what you get instead — so either SiteSnap isn’t installed on this iPhone yet, or you’re reading your email on a computer or an Android phone.

SiteSnap is an iPhone app. There is no Android or web version yet.

How to accept

  1. SiteSnap is in limited release and isn’t on the public App Store yet. Reply to the person who invited you and ask them to send you a TestFlight invitation.
  2. Install the app on your iPhone, then open this email again on that iPhone and tap Accept Invitation.
  3. Sign in, or create an account, using the same email address the invitation was sent to. An invitation can only be accepted by that address.

Invitations expire 7 days after they are sent. If yours has expired, ask for a new one — nothing is lost.

Already have SiteSnap on this iPhone?

This link looks incomplete

The invitation details are missing from the address, which usually means the link was copied by hand or an email app shortened it. Open the original invitation email and tap Accept Invitation rather than typing the address in.
