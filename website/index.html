What it does

One place for the records a job actually generates.

No feature inflation — this is what SiteSnap captures and keeps, described plainly.

Site diaries

Daily entries — weather, crew, work done, notes — with photos attached. AI can draft the write-up from what's on site; a person reviews it before it counts.

Incident records

Structured incident and near-miss reporting laid out around WorkSafe NZ's fields, so the record you keep matches what an investigation asks for.

Inspections with sign-off

Checklist inspections with per-item results and photo evidence, closed out with a signature captured on the device.

Timesheets

Crew hours logged against the site and day — start, finish, breaks and trade — ready to review and export.

Delivery dockets

Record what arrived on site — supplier, items, quantities — with the docket photographed and filed against the job.

Photo evidence

Photos are attached to the record they belong to and kept as the originals — the picture stays tied to the diary, incident or inspection it documents.

Why SiteSnap

Made for the way sites here actually run.

Three things it's deliberately built around, rather than bolted on.

NZ compliance structure

Records that match the framework

Incident reporting is structured around WorkSafe NZ's grouping, and diary records are kept with construction record-keeping obligations in mind — so the record fits the framework you're already working to.

  • WorkSafe-aligned incident fields
  • Built for NZ & AU sites
Offline reliability

Works where the signal doesn't

Sites lose coverage. Capture keeps working in the field, and the letterhead and logo on exported documents are built in — so a report reads correctly even with no connection at the time.

  • Capture on site, sync when you're back
  • Exports render without a live connection
Evidence integrity

The record you can rely on later

The original photo is preserved when it's marked up, so the source image is never overwritten. Sign-offs are immutable — a completed signature can't be quietly edited; it's voided and re-signed, and that history is kept.

  • Originals preserved on annotation
  • Immutable, void-and-re-sign signatures
